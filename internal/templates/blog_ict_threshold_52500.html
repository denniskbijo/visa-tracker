The general salary floor for the Senior or Specialist Worker visa (the route people still call ICT) has been £52,500 since 22 July 2025, under Statement of Changes HC 997. This site was still showing £48,500. The monthly gov.uk check caught it, the figure is fixed, and the data is now checked as of 15 November 2026. From now on that check updates the date itself when nothing has moved, and flags me when something has.

If this is your route, use £52,500 or the going rate for your job, whichever is higher. This is a guide, not advice.
