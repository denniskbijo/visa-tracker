# The ICT floor is £52,500. This site still said £48,500.

**Date:** 2026-11-15

The general salary floor for the Senior or Specialist Worker visa (the route people still call ICT) has been £52,500 since 22 July 2025, under [Statement of Changes HC 997](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-997-1-july-2025/statement-of-changes-to-the-immigration-rules-hc-997-1-july-2025-accessible-version). This site was still showing £48,500. The monthly gov.uk check caught it, the figure is fixed, and the data is now checked as of 15 November 2026. From now on that check updates the date itself when nothing has moved, and flags me when something has.
